Frequently asked

Quick answers.

Is it free?

The beta is completely free. When we launch on the App Store, there will be a free tier with limited features and a premium plan starting at $9.99/month.

What phones does it work on?

iPhone only right now (iOS 15+). Android is planned for later this year. Join the waitlist below to get notified.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can start using the app immediately as a guest. Creating an account saves your data across devices and unlocks household features.

How does the kitchen scan work?

Point your phone camera at your fridge, pantry, or counter. AI identifies what you have in seconds — including quantities and expiry estimates. Then recommendations are based on what's actually in your kitchen.

What about food allergies?

We take this very seriously. The engine checks every ingredient against 7 allergen categories (shellfish, gluten, dairy, nuts, eggs, soy, fish) with 100+ keyword mappings. If anyone in your household has an allergy, no unsafe meal will ever be suggested.
