Allergen safety

Ingredient-level checks across 7 categories. If anyone in your household has a shellfish allergy, no meal with shrimp, crab, or lobster will ever appear. Not reduced in ranking - completely excluded.

Household harmony

She's gluten-free, he hates cilantro, the kids won't eat fish. The engine only suggests meals that genuinely work for everyone. No compromises. No arguments.

Real questions, real answers

Before you try it.

Is it free?

The beta is completely free. When we launch, there will be a free tier with core features and a premium plan at $9.99/month for unlimited recommendations, all 8 voices, and full weekly planning.

What is TestFlight?

TestFlight is Apple's official beta testing app. It's made by Apple, safe to use, and free. You install TestFlight from the App Store, then tap our link to get Soréll. Takes about 2 minutes.

Does it work for one person, not just couples?

Absolutely. Solo cooks get the same scan-to-cook experience. The household features just mean it works even better when more people are involved.

How is this different from Mealime or Paprika?

Those apps help you browse recipes and plan manually. Soréll decides for you. It scans what you actually have, accounts for everyone's dietary needs, and gives you one clear answer - not a list of 200 recipes to scroll through.

What about food allergies?

We check every ingredient against 7 allergen categories with 100+ keyword mappings. If anyone in your household has an allergy, no unsafe meal will ever be suggested. This is a hard exclusion - not a warning, not a filter you can forget to set.
