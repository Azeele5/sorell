Common questions.

Is Soréll a calorie tracker?

No. Soréll answers a different question: what should you cook tonight? It scans your kitchen, considers who's eating, and recommends a meal that fits - instead of counting calories after the fact.

Does Soréll work without a subscription?

Yes. The free tier includes three kitchen scans per week and unlimited meal recommendations from your existing kitchen. Premium adds unlimited scans and the intelligence layer (cuisine rotation, cooking fatigue, ingredient synergy).

How does Soréll handle billing?

Through Apple In-App Purchase, managed by RevenueCat. No Stripe checkouts, no out-of-band payment flows, no surprise charges. Apple's standard 14-day refund and one-tap cancellation rules apply.

Can I use both Cal AI and Soréll?

Yes. They solve different problems. Cal AI tracks what you ate; Soréll decides what to cook. Many people use a tracker and a planner side by side - one looks backward, one looks forward.

What about my privacy?

Account deletion cascades through every table that holds your data. Database access is locked at the row level - even if our server were breached, no user could read another user's data through the app. We don't sell behavioral data, ever.
